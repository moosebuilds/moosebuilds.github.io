Moose Builds it does not pulse

Privacy

The apps and integrations registered under this site (for example the NjordHealth Fitbit connector) are personal, single-user tools. They are run by, and for, their author only.

What is collected

Only the account owner's own data, read from the services the owner has explicitly connected (such as Fitbit health and sleep metrics via Google's OAuth flow). No other person's data is accessed, and nothing is collected from visitors to this site.

Where it goes

Data is stored on the owner's own private hardware at home. It is not sold, shared, published, or sent to any third party, and no analytics or advertising services are used.

Removal

Access can be revoked at any time from the connected service's own account settings, after which no further data is read. Questions: github.com/moosebuilds.
